# HujraCart by Khan's AI

**Every shop on one map.** Sales, inventory and shop discovery for shopkeepers, field salesmen and customers, on Android and Windows.

HujraCart is launched and in daily use by shops, salesmen and customers.

## Install

**Android**
1. Download the APK on your phone and tap it.
2. If the phone asks, allow installing apps from this source (Chrome or Files).
3. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**.
4. Open HujraCart and sign in.

**Windows**
1. Download the zip, right-click it > **Extract All**.
2. Open the `HujraCart` folder and double-click `HujraCart.exe`. (Tip: right-click it > Send to > Desktop to make a shortcut.)
3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** > **Run anyway**.

## What it does

- **Customers:** find the nearest shop that has what you need, search a product, get walking directions.
- **Salesmen:** visit shops, record sales on the spot and print or share the receipt.
- **Shopkeepers:** manage stock, deliveries in, sales records and insights, from a phone or a counter PC.
- **Reports:** any period, exported as PDF.
